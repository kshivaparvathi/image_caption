About Image Caption AI

Image Caption AI is built using authentic open-source multimodal neural models (Salesforce BLIP and BLIP VQA). It directly scans pixel structures to detect living subjects, environments, activities, and lighting nuances.

🔒 Complete Privacy Runs on open-source deep learning without external cloud vision dependencies.
🌐 Authentic Indian & Global Languages Generates captions natively in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, and more.
🎙️ Interactive Speech Integrated browser voice recognition and text-to-speech with full Play, Pause, and Stop controls.
